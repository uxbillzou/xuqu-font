**Super short description of the Font Family:**

Xuqu is a wide geometric Latin display family with cut corners and angular joins,
developed from the XUQU Tech wordmark for branding, posters and video titles.
It includes five static upright weights: Thin 100, Light 300, Regular 400,
Bold 700 and ExtraBold 800. The app-menu family name is **Xuqu**.

**AI use and provenance:**

The user supplied the XUQU Tech wordmark and directed the family design. Custom
outlines were created through AI-assisted Python/FontTools/Shapely engineering;
the recorded process did not copy third-party font outlines. Approved A/B
letterforms, legacy generation code and the exact v1.3 regression references are
included. See [PROVENANCE.md](https://github.com/uxbillzou/xuqu-font/blob/main/documentation/PROVENANCE.md).

**Version, coverage and QA:**

- Current source candidate: **1.402**, branch **main**.
- 400 encoded characters and 416 glyphs per style; no CJK, italics or variable axes.
