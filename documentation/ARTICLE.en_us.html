Xuqu is a wide geometric Latin display typeface developed from the XUQU Tech wordmark. Its angular joins, cut corners and open geometric forms suit branding, posters and video titles.

The family includes Thin, Light, Regular, Bold and ExtraBold upright styles. The Latin character set includes accented letters and combining marks, with proportional default figures and tabular figures available through OpenType. Thin styles are intended primarily for larger display sizes.

The project was developed with design direction from Xu Zou / 邹旭（序曲） and AI-assisted custom font engineering. It includes editable UFO sources and a reproducible fontmake build.
